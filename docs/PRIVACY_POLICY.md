# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Full name
- Company name
- Password (encrypted)

### Lead Data
- Email addresses uploaded by users
- Names, job titles, company information
- LinkedIn profiles (public data only)

### Usage Data
- Campaign statistics (opens, clicks, replies)
- API usage logs
- Device information

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve our services
- Send transactional emails
- Generate AI-powered personalization
- Analytics and reporting
- Customer support

## 3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- **Email service providers** (SendGrid, SES, Mailgun) — to send your campaigns
- **Lead data APIs** (Apollo.io, Hunter.io) — to find leads you request
- **OpenAI** — to generate personalized content (data is not used for training)

## 4. Data Security

- All passwords are bcrypt-hashed
- API keys are encrypted at rest
- HTTPS encryption in transit
- Regular security audits

## 5. Your Rights

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Delete:** Request deletion of your account and data
- **Export:** Download your leads and campaign data as CSV
- **Opt-out:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails

## 6. CAN-SPAM Compliance

All emails sent through our platform include:
- Unsubscribe links
- Physical mailing address
- Clear sender identification

## 7. GDPR Compliance

For EU users:
- Right to access, rectification, erasure
- Data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Data Processing Agreement available

## 8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for:
- Authentication (JWT tokens)
- Session management
- Analytics (anonymized)

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 16.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Users will be notified of significant changes.
